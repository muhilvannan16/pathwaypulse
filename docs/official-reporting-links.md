# Official reporting links for Rochester Hills
To report a damaged, blocked, or missing sidewalk or pathway in Rochester Hills, use the City of Rochester Hills “Report a Concern” page (linked below). PathwayPulse does not send reports to the city.

## Where to report the issue

Report a damaged, obstructed, or otherwise unsafe sidewalk or pathway in Rochester Hills to the **City of Rochester Hills**:

- [Report a Concern](https://rochesterhills.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/reporter/index.html?appid=fa2a4da97b254606a231c14d7647b79b) — the City’s online map-based reporting form.
- Department of Public Services: **248-656-4685** · [dps@rochesterhills.org](mailto:dps@rochesterhills.org)

Include the precise location (street address or nearest intersections), a description of the damage or blockage, and photos when available.

## Who is responsible?

The **City of Rochester Hills** is the appropriate first contact for sidewalk and pathway concerns, including paths alongside a county road. The Road Commission for Oakland County (RCOC) states on its official issue-reporting form: **“For sidewalk and path issues, contact your city, village or township.”**

RCOC is the reporting route for issues with the **county road itself**, such as pavement, shoulders, drainage, signs, or a roadside obstruction. Its [Report an Issue form](https://www.rcocweb.org/FormCenter/Contact-Us-4/Report-An-Issue-in-OAKLAND-COUNTY-MICHIG-43) also links to road-jurisdiction information.

## Emergency road hazards

For an emergency road condition, RCOC says not to use its web form. Call **877-858-4804** on weekdays from 7:30 a.m.–4:15 p.m. (excluding holidays), or **248-858-4895** after hours and on weekends.
